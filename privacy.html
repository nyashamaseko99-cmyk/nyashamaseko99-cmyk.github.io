Privacy policy

Last updated 5 November 2026.

Legends of Africa channel tools is a private tool that uses YouTube API Services. It is used only for channels whose owners have signed in and approved access.

What it accesses

With that approval it can upload videos, update video details and comments, and read channel analytics for the approved channel. It doesn't collect information about viewers.

How the data is used and stored

Channel data is used only to publish and measure that channel's videos. Sign-in tokens and reports are stored on the owner's own computer. The data isn't sold. Channel reports may be processed by the AI assistant services the owner uses to run the tool, and they aren't shared with anyone else.

Removing access

A channel owner can remove access at any time at Google account permissions. To have stored data deleted, write to the address below.

Google and YouTube

By using this tool you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.
